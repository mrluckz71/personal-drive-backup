Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Data Access

Personal Drive Backup uses Google OAuth authorization to access Google Drive files from accounts explicitly authorized by their owners.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is accessed solely to create personal backups using rclone.

Data Storage

Downloaded files are stored on the user's local storage. OAuth credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

The application operator does not sell or share Google Drive data with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files remain on local storage until manually deleted. Users may revoke Google access through their Google Account settings.
